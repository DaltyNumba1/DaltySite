This project combines obscure fashion with 3D, audio, and video work. It started with an idea that it would be a cool look to have deer antlers as a person. So I decided to turn that idea into something real.
I attached real deer antlers to a leather headband I crafted and pulled a knit hat over the top to give the illusion that I really had antlers growing out of my head. I created this video as a way to model the piece.
